# Asking ArtScroll for the files (draft for Josh to send)

ArtScroll has licensed its texts to RabbAI (Josh, 2026-11-17). Unlike Koren, ArtScroll's texts
are not published openly anywhere online: the only digital copies are ArtScroll's own Digital
Library app, which is locked, and copies other people posted without ArtScroll's leave. RabbAI
doesn't take text from either (CLAUDE.md: never get around a site's protections), so the texts
need to come from ArtScroll. Publishers that run digital editions keep their books as files
(XML, EPUB or the files behind their app), and sending them is usually simple for them.

---

**Subject:** RabbAI: receiving the licensed ArtScroll texts

Dear [name],

Thank you again for licensing ArtScroll's texts to RabbAI. To bring them into the app, we need
the texts as files. Could you send them in whatever form is easiest for you, for example:

- the XML or EPUB files behind the ArtScroll Digital Library, or
- the typesetting files (InDesign or similar) for the books, or
- any export with the Hebrew, the English and the notes kept apart.

The books we would start with:

1. The Schottenstein Talmud Bavli (English and Hebrew), with its notes.
2. The ArtScroll Siddur (Ashkenaz and Sefard), including the interlinear edition.
3. The Stone Chumash and the Rubin Edition of the Prophets and Writings.
4. The Schottenstein Mishnah Berurah (Dirshu notes, if included).

Two things we would like to have in writing with the files, so our records are complete:

- whether the licence covers a public app later, or private use for now;
- how ArtScroll would like the texts credited wherever they are shown.

The files will be kept privately, used only inside RabbAI, and never published as files.

With thanks,
[your name]
RabbAI

---
