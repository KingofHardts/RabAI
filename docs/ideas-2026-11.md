# Ideas for the chat and the learning (2026-11-13)

Josh liked keeping answers to the suggested questions, a children's mode and sharing answers, and
asked for more ideas "that could be cool and unique and very good and intuitive and effective, in
that kind of outside of the box way". **Built** means done on 2026-11-13. **Next** means small and
planned. **Later** means more work. **Board** means it touches what RabbAI says about Torah, so it
waits for the rabbinic board.

## Built

1. **Kept answers to the suggested questions.** The first time one is asked in a fresh chat, its
   checked answer is kept for a week, and the next person who taps it gets it at once, marked with
   the day it was written and "Ask again for a fresh one" (`web/lib/kept-answers.ts`). Practical
   halacha answers are always fresh. Nothing about the person is kept.
2. **Learning with a child.** A switch in Settings, and a note beside the question box while it is
   on. RabbAI answers for a child of about eight to twelve (core premises "Teaching a child", a
   draft for the board, open question 28).
3. **"Explain it for a child"** under any answer, for one answer, without turning the mode on.
4. **A picture of an answer**, to send on WhatsApp or by text: the question, the answer, its
   sources and "From RabbAI, an AI Torah teacher, not a rav", made on the device.
5. **A source sheet.** The texts an answer cited, in Hebrew and English as the library has them,
   on a page ready to print: for a shiur, a Shabbat table or a chavrusa.
6. **The daily learning you follow.** Choose your cycles (Mishnah Yomit, Rambam, Nach Yomi,
   Mishnah Berurah, Kitzur, Tanakh Yomi, Tanya…); today's portion of each shows on the Learn tab
   with "Open" (straight to the library's text) and "Learn it" (with RabbAI).

## Next

7. **For the Shabbat table.** On Thursday and Friday, a two-minute dvar Torah on the parsha to say
   at the table, with one question for the children and a source sheet to print.
8. **Before you go.** Ask RabbAI what to learn before a shiur, a wedding (the sheva brachot), a
   bris or a yahrzeit, and get the three or four texts to look at, as a source sheet.
9. **Your own questions, saved as a sefer.** A saved chat category becomes a printable booklet:
   each question with its answer and sources, like a book of she'elot you asked over a year.
10. **"I learned this today."** One tap after a chavrusa session saves what was learned and its
    texts, and in three days RabbAI offers a two-question review (spaced review, as yeshivos do
    with chazara).
11. **Chazara mode.** "Review what I learned this week": RabbAI asks, the person answers, and it
    says how they did, from the texts.
12. **The daf in one picture.** "Show the flow" as a shareable picture of the page's argument
    (already labeled as RabbAI's outline, not yet reviewed).
13. **A question from the parsha for each night of the week**, for families: short, with the
    answer folded until tapped.
14. **Learn while you walk.** Talk with the screen off: RabbAI reads a line, asks what it means,
    and waits, like a chavrusa on a phone call.
15. **Bookmarks with a note.** Mark a line in the reader with your own note; the note is yours,
    on your device, and RabbAI can quiz you on your marked lines.

## Later

16. **A group chavrusa.** Two people on two phones learning the same text with RabbAI, each seeing
    the other's questions (needs accounts and a shared session).
17. **Teacher's corner.** For rebbeim and morot: a lesson plan for a text, with questions by age
    and a source sheet (needs the board to look at the lesson format).
18. **Siyum help.** When someone finishes a masechta or a sefer, the hadran text, a short dvar
    Torah linking the end to the beginning, and a source sheet for the siyum.
19. **Yahrzeit learning.** On a yahrzeit the person entered (kept on their device), Mishnayot
    whose chapters spell the name, as is customary, opened in the library.
20. **Hear it from the page.** Point the camera at a printed line; RabbAI finds it in the library
    and reads it with you (pictures already find the page; this makes it live).
21. **A shiur you can ask.** A recorded shiur from TorahAnytime (with its permission) beside the
    daf, and RabbAI answering questions on the texts it cites.
22. **Calendar learning plans.** "Finish Mesillat Yesharim by Rosh Hashanah": a daily portion,
    reminders the person adds to their own calendar, and the cycle on the Learn tab.

## Board

23. **Kept answers for more than the suggested questions** (the same answer twice for any
    question many people ask) changes how RabbAI answers people: the board decides whether that
    is wanted.
24. **A daf summary.** Decided for now (2026-11-13): no summaries of a daf are shown. RabbAI's own
    is not a source, and the library has none from a teacher; "Show the flow" stays, labeled.
25. **The children's section** in the core premises, and whether it should have a younger and an
    older level (open question 28).
26. **Answering in a child's language for halacha**: what a child may be told directly and what is
    always for a parent or rav.
