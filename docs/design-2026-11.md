# Making RabbAI the best it can be: the lists (November 2026)

Josh asked (2026-11-10) for a hard look at the whole app: fix the order of the morning prayers,
organize the Daven tab properly, make every screen easier to get around, and make RabbAI itself
sharper, faster and truer to what it is meant to be. He asked for lists: 100 ways to improve the
chat, 100 for the Learn tab, 100 for the Daven tab, 50 for the top bar, 100 for how smart and
refined the AI is, about 50 for its speed, and 100 for its character as a teacher, a chavrusa and a
companion to the siddur.

Every item says where it stands:

- **Built** — done on 2026-11-10: in the first round, or, where it says so, in the second round the same day (the "Next" items Josh asked for).
- **Have** — already in the app before this round.
- **Next** — small and planned.
- **Later** — worth doing, needs more work.
- **Ask** — needs a decision from Josh or the rabbinic board.

Counts at the end. The lists were written after looking at every screen on a phone and a computer,
with the real list of books and the real sections of the four siddurim.

## E. A sharper, more refined AI (100)

### Answering well (new instructions, drafts for the board)

1. The first sentence is the answer. **Built.**
2. The shape fits the question: a fact, a why, a halacha, a text, a hard situation, a dispute. **Built.**
3. When a question could mean two things, say which was answered. **Built.**
4. Ask first only when the two answers would be very different. **Built.**
5. Say how settled a thing is: all agree, most, a dispute, one view. **Built.**
6. Never make a minority view sound accepted. **Built.**
7. Never turn a custom into a law or a law into a custom. **Built.**
8. Name the source in the sentence that uses it. **Built.**
9. One clear example. **Built.**
10. Nothing extra: no restated question, no closing summary. **Built.**
11. Build on the conversation; don't re-explain. **Built.**
12. When corrected, look again; agree plainly or show the words kindly. **Built.**
13. Offer one next step. **Built.**
14. Short by default, more on request. **Have.**
15. Translate a term the first time. **Have.**
16. Meet the person at their level. **Have.**

### Truth and sources

17. Every source shown passes a check against the text. **Have.**
18. The words marked while it writes pass the same check. **Have.**
19. A line read aloud is the library's, never the model's copy. **Have.**
20. Only the whitelist is read. **Have.**
21. A debated book carries its caution. **Have.**

## The "Next" items that are held back, and why

Josh asked (2026-11-10) for every "Next" item to be built. The second round built the ones that
are safe to build without measuring or asking. The other 47 are held for one of these reasons:

- **They change which texts RabbAI opens, or which model answers** (F12, F13, F15, F16, F17, F32,
  E41, E56, E57). A wrong shortcut here makes answers worse, quietly. Each one needs to be tried on
  the live app with the health check before and after, one at a time.
- **They are halacha, and belong to the board** (A9, A10, A82, A83): what is skipped on Shabbat,
  hiding tefillin, what is said without a minyan, the leader's parts. The app may fold a line with
  a label and never remove one, and what to fold is the board's to confirm (open question 23).
- **They need something the app doesn't have yet**: Hebrew names for the outline that someone has
  checked (A26), a line about each book from the canon (B46, B38), flashcards and review reminders
  (B81, B84), voice search (B28).
- **They are larger pieces of screen work** that deserve their own round and a look on real phones:
  one-hand mode (A57), a first path for beginners (B16), sharing a line (B59), a "where am I" line
  (B61), the phone's back gesture (B92), print and "sources only" views of an answer (C52, C54),
  chats grouped by date and sessions listed apart (C61, C64), a full-screen Talk view, "repeat
  that" and speech speed (C73, C75, C76), a first-time tour and a "what's new" note (C95, C96), the
  simple / usual / deep switch (C26), settings as a full page on a phone (D33), the smaller bar
  items (D18, D19, D35, D38, D40, D48), opening a prayer in Daven from an answer (E80), more names
  for the voice (E72), transliteration by the person's choice in the chat (E75), remembering an
  open question (G56), and the two tests of manner (G98, G99), which need the live model.
- **Stop writing when you cut in** (F37) touches the path that keeps an answer after the page
  leaves, so it needs care that a stopped answer still keeps nothing.
